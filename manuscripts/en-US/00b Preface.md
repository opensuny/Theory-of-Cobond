# Preface

**The Cruel Reality**
In 2026, the Russia-Ukraine war has entered its fifth year. Both sides have suffered enormous losses and continue to be drained, with peace still nowhere in sight. Everyone is talking about the situation, victory or defeat, ceasefires, and sanctions, but a fundamental problem remains: the existing peace proposals—victory for one side, a frozen front line, maintaining neutrality, or security guarantees from a third party—all have structural flaws and fail to resolve the root causes of the conflict. They merely redistribute the crumbs of a zero-sum pie and serve as a prelude to the next, even more intense conflict. This exposes the structural flaws in the current international security architecture—an insoluble stalemate and deadlock—unless we reconstruct the security architecture itself.

Meanwhile, almost all countries around the world are rapidly expanding their military forces, Although this seemingly enhances their own security, it has instead caused other countries to experience deeper security anxiety, forcing them to also expand their military forces. This, in turn, has triggered a new round of global arms race. The risk of large-scale conflict and war is increasingly intensifying — this is the direct manifestation of the current international security framework is on the verge of collapse.

The international relations are in a state of anarchy and prioritize interests. This is the realistic starting point for the theoretical development of this book. Humans have attempted four international order models so far, but all have failed or are on the verge of collapse:
①Moral appeal (liberalism and idealism) - Speed limit sign
Relying on universal morality, international conventions, and ethical condemnation, it calls on countries to restrain aggression, from Kant's "Perpetual Peace Theory" to the UN Charter. Humans once hoped that reason, international law, and democratic systems could curb wars. But history has proved that when facing the brutal assault of power, it completely failed.
②Mutual assured destruction (realism, Mutually Assured Destruction, MAD) - Armored car racing and collision
Relying on superior military power, destructive weapons, and confrontational military alliances to form a reciprocal deterrent, this is intended to curb direct wars between major powers. However, the side effects are extremely severe: it leads to an endless military competition and an Arms Black Hole, forcing countries to continuously expend their national resources, devour human wealth, and destroy populations, thereby triggering widespread security anxiety and survival crises among humanity.
③World government (centralized hegemony and empire) - Steel and concrete gate
Empires in history, the Soviet Union in the modern era, and the unipolar order dominated by the United States in the contemporary era, all attempted to rule the world with centralized power.  Eventually, they all ended up in the same outcome: oppression, disasters, collapse, and disintegration.
④Economic Interdependence (Commercial Peace Theory) - Trailer Hitch Rope
It relies on free trade, energy interconnection, and financial binding to maintain peace. When moving smoothly, it achieves mutual benefit and win-win results. However, once there is a geopolitical conflict, it instantly breaks down, dragging both sides into the abyss. Commercial profits are vulnerable in the face of "survival security" and mutual economic dependence eventually turns into a weaponized bargaining chip.

The tombstones of these four paradigms have been erected on the bloody ruins of the Russia-Ukraine war. War has plagued humanity for thousands of years. Moral appeal and international treaties are like empty words. Zero-sum games are intensifying. Must humans be repeatedly imprisoned and tortured in the cycle of "weakness, mutual annihilation, and totalitarian rule"?

No, this book designs a new type of inter-state relationship form from an engineering perspective, called "Cobond" - that is, a security and destiny community composed of independent sovereign entities through the sharing of core functions. This is the fifth new security paradigm:
⑤Mutual assured constraint (Mutually Assured Constraint, MAC) - Speed Bump
Its core proposition is both radical and clear: War cannot be permanently ended through unilateral victory, sanctions, or ceasefire treaties. Only by designing a completely new structural design for the security relationship between countries can fundamental changes be achieved. Its core framework is "sovereign independence + military interlocking": Member states retain complete independent sovereignty and deeply share interlocking in the security field. From a physical structure perspective, war loses its meaning. The deceleration belt does not prohibit passage, but the cost of a high-speed collision is far higher than orderly passage - violent jolting, direction loss, vehicle damage.

**Cobond: A New Form for Human Civilization**
Cobond (broad sense): A form of symbiotic relationship composed of multiple independent entities—that is, a community of security and shared destiny formed by shared core functions. It refers to a universal law governing the combination of all things in the universe, describing the combinatorial logic by which independent entities (such as particles, cells, living organisms, organizations, civilizations, and planets) interlock and connect through deep-seated rules to form a higher-level, stable system.
Cobond (narrow sense: International Politics): A symbiotic relationship structure formed by multiple independent entities, with shared interlocking in the security domain and independence in all other domains.

**Three Fundamental Axioms**
① Axiom of Survival: The primary purpose of life is survival and continuity.
② Axiom of Form Serving: Form serves individual life; Civilizational form serves individual human beings; State form serves the people.
③ Axiom of Perfect Paradox: Imperfection is the foundation of existence and the driving force behind evolution.

**Four Architectural Pillars**
① Sovereign Independence: Each maintains its own system, independent and free.
② Military Interlocking: Shared, mixed-formation forces; symbiotic security.
③ Social Integration: Intertwined interests; symbiotic development.
④ Covenant of Civilization Perpetual Prosperity: Cyclical hedging; perpetual sustainability of civilization.
